# Project #1 - Comparison of 2 input values
A combination of logic gates and units to evaluate two single-bit values determining their differences and similarities in magnitude. 

Difficulty of Code: Easy

Purpose: Functions to compare 2 inputs, detecting if values are equal to, less than, or greater than, helping as a way to sort and compare larger numbers if produced larger scale.

## Logic Formulas:
- Equal (A==B with both either being 0 or 1 value) (XNOR function)
- Greater than (A>B with A being 1 and B being 0) (AND + NOT B function)
- Less than (A<B with A being 0 and B being 1) (AND + NOT A function)
