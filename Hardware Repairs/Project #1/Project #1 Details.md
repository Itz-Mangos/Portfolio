# Project #1: Xbox 360 Deep Clean
Model: Xbox 360 250GB (Jasper Revision)

Difficulty of Cleaning Process: Medium

Purpose: Full-Restoration of Console Through Deep Cleaning

## Parts Cleaned:
- Structure and Enclosure - Plastic Chassis & Metal RF Shielding
- Thermal Management - Dual-Exhaust Fan Array & Aluminum Heat Sinks
- Processor Core - Thermal Paste Replacement for the Loki and Zeus chips
- Inputs/Outputs - HDMI, USB, and the Power Receiver Ports
- Motherboard - All surface level corrosion with Capacitors

## Workflow and Steps:
- Deconstruct - Full tear-down of the system, from the shell to the Motherboard
- Chemical Wash - Stripping down buildup of oxidant layers through 99% Isopropyl Alcohol and Compressed Air
- Thermal Overhaul - Complete removal of degraded thermal paste with replacement of fresh, high conductive compound
- Structural Polish - Treatment of stubborn spots with anti corrosive sprays and revision of all spots for proper cleaning

## Materials Used:
- Screwdriver Set (Torx T8 and T10 Screws)
- Plastic Spudger
- Thermal Paste Compound
- 100% Isopropyl Alcohol
- Microfiber Cloth
- Compressed Air/Electric Air Dusters
